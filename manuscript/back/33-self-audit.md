# The Self-Audit Scorecard

Rate yourself twice on each law: once now, before the first lap
ends, and once after lap three. Use the 1 to 5 scale with real
anchors: 1 means the law has never consciously occurred to you;
3 means you ran it sometimes this quarter; 5 means you ran it in
its full form for most of a month, on schedule, without
prompting. The gap between the two columns is your own map of
where the third lap goes.

Scoring notes, so the numbers stay honest. Rate conduct, not
conviction: a law you believe in and never run is a 1. Do the
"Now" column in ink before you finish rereading part one, and do
not discuss the ratings with anyone; this page is the one place
in the book where comparisons are forbidden, since the whole
audience for the two columns is you. When you retake the audit
after lap three, expect roughly four laws to move and the rest
to hold; the four that moved are your code, and the two lowest
scores consign the next lap's aim.
