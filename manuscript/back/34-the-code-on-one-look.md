# The Code on One Look

The daily cycle's ninety seconds live here: today's law, read
once, done today. Openers only; the chapters sit behind them.

1. **The Right to Be Rich.** Before any other step, give
   yourself permission to want.
2. **Getting Rich Is Not Luck; It Is Law.** Riches obey
   arithmetic, not astrology.
3. **Opportunity Is Not Monopolized.** You are not late.
4. **Think Like an Owner.** You are built to increase; plan
   accordingly.
5. **See It Before You Have It.** The picture comes before the
   permission slip from the world; write yours in full color.
6. **The Gratitude Switch.** Gratitude is bookkeeping for
   signals: it keeps your attention on what is working.
7. **Mind Your Own Mind.** The will is for keeping your own
   attention on your picture, never for bending theirs.
8. **Do All That Can Be Done Today.** You can act only where you
   are, and only on what today holds.
9. **Do Not Mistake Your Vocation.** Select work your nature can
   actually stand inside of for ten thousand hours.
10. **Put Yourself Where the Demand Is.** Right trade, wrong
    town, starves; the demand has an address, and your business
    must live on it.
11. **Debt Bites Slow and Holds Hard.** Debt takes the evenings,
    the sleep, and eventually the self; price it as a full-time
    tenant.
12. **Whatever Is Worth Doing, Take Your Whole Self.** Half-effort
    is the most expensive thing you will ever buy, because it
    costs everything and completes nothing.
13. **Use the Best Tools, Including People.** Cheap tools and
    cheap people are the two most expensive purchases in
    business.
14. **Serve Your Trade, Not Your Vanity.** The trade you look
    down on today is the floor you will stand on in your worst
    decade.
15. **Hope Much, Bet Little.** Optimism is fuel; sound bets come
    from the evidence, not the fuel.
16. **A Time and a Place for Everything.** System is not
    stiffness; it is the machine that buys your evenings back.
17. **Side Bets Sink Ships.** Your signature is your fortune's
    hull; outside operations and loose endorsements are the
    water.
18. **Make the Public Look Your Way.** A genuine article nobody
    has heard of is a museum after closing; advertising is the
    hours on the door.
19. **Leave Everyone a Little Better.** Convey the impression of
    increase: every deal and every encounter should leave the
    other side richer for having met you.
20. **Get Into the Right Business.** Fit beats talent, desire is
    the proof of power, and the wrong desk is escaped by growth
    rather than by leap.
21. **Integrity Is the Fortune That Cannot Be Lost.** Suspicion
    of false weights closes every door at once; a clean name
    borrows the whole community's purse.
22. **The Whole Code in One Hand.** Twenty-two laws, thirty
    days, four laps: the code is small enough to carry and
    heavy enough to live.

One lap through this page, one act per line, is a month. Four
laps is a habit with the year's receipts attached.
