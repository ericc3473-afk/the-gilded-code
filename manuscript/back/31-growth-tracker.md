# The Growth Tracker

One row per law, four laps per row. Mark the box when you have
*lived* the law for a full day: read its opener, ran its do-now,
logged its track line. A half-day counts as nothing on this
sheet. Print it or copy it into your notebook, and when a lap
completes, date the lap column.
